# GlycoTwin ML Research

Research, dataset analysis, and technical foundations
for the machine learning components of GlycoTwin.

## Overview

This directory contains the research behind GlycoTwin's
machine learning system, including dataset analysis,
comparisons, schema investigations, and research references.

## Research Objectives

Our research focuses on three major areas:

1. Diabetes Risk Classification
2. Blood Glucose Forecasting
3. Personalised Digital Twin Context

## Research Areas

### 1. Diabetes Risk Classification
- Clinical and demographic feature analysis
- Dataset comparison
- Classification research

### 2. Blood Glucose Forecasting
- Glucose time-series analysis
- Temporal dataset investigation
- Forecasting research

### 3. Digital Twin Context
- Patient health records
- Lifestyle and contextual information
- Personalisation research

## Dataset Research

| Dataset | Research Purpose |
|---|---|
| NHANES | Clinical and demographic analysis |

| UK Biobank | Large-scale health research |
| T1DEXI | Glucose and activity research |
| DiaTrend | Glucose time-series research |
| OhioT1DM | Diabetes time-series research |
| Synthea | Synthetic patient records |

## Important Notes

This directory documents research and dataset evaluation.
Dataset investigation does not necessarily mean that
the dataset has been downloaded, processed, or used
for model training.
